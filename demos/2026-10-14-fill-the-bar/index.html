🤓 Nerd notes For researchers: real terms, numbers, paper

Last round, in real terms: Play a level to see the expert note here.

Word map: Φ = progress score 0–1 · Submit = claim the job is done · PPRS = R = 1[pass] + λ Φ(s_T) with λ=0.25 · GRPO = compare a batch of tries · Sparse = only Pass/Fail · Dense = mid-job progress signal.

The paper. Tev: Scaling Long-Horizon Agentic Environments with Dense Progress Rewards (arXiv 2610.18868; tev-agent.github.io). Tev-Gym: 1,170 verified envs across 7 domains; median agent time 12.4 min; Gemini 3.6 Flash P@1 29%. Tev-27B ALE-CM P@5 0.28 (base Qwen3.8-27B 0.17, +65%), matching GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5 at Pass@5. TB 4.0-CM P@1: 0.04→0.10 (+150%); TB 3.0-CM 0.09→0.14 (+56%); TB 2.1 P@1 0.794. PPRS: R = 1[pass] + λ Φ(s_T) with λ=0.25; sparse GRPO wastes (1-p)^G groups (66% at p=0.05, G=8). Skeptic: matches frontier Pass@5 on ALE-CM but TB 4.0 still 0.10 vs Claude Opus 5 at 0.52.

How the game maps to it. You are the trainer. Level 1 forces mid-job Φ feeds before Submit (sparse Submit-only = zero mid signal = lose). Level 2 adds a turn budget and regressions that drop Φ when you tap the wrong stage. Level 3 is a GRPO group of 8: sparse Pass/Fail wastes the whole group; dense PPRS ranks by Φ so you get a gradient.

Toy parts. Stage layout, turn budgets, and on-screen Φ steps are hand-written. Research-card numbers are from the paper only.
