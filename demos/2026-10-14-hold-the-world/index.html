How the game maps to it. The first-person path is a toy world rollout. Drift meter is a toy stand-in for long-horizon identity / color / geometry failure. FORCE LONG LOOK spends budget to extend the teacher horizon (the paper's longer DMD supervision). Level 1 teaches that an 81-frame (~5s) look lets the world crash. Level 2 makes you pick a horizon with paper clues (action and drift numbers). Level 3 is a 45s roam: action rises only under a long look when drift is controlled; spam drains budget; ignoring drift crashes. Skeptic (Prop 4.1): short windows do not identify long paths; longer supervision slows drift but does not erase it.

Toy parts. Path art, drift speed curve, budget counts, and the 45s clock are hand-written. Research-card numbers are from the paper only.
