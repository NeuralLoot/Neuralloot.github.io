Nerd notes (sources)
  • Model: GPT-2 small (openai-community/gpt2, 124M parameters, 50,257-token vocabulary), run on our own computer. Every chance on this page comes from its real output scores for the sentence so far, saved to a file. No live AI runs in your browser.
  • Heat = temperature T. Chance of word i = exp(z_i / T) / sum of exp(z_j / T) over all 50,257 tokens, where z are the model's scores (logits). Hinton, Vinyals and Dean 2015, eq. 1 (arXiv 1503.02531). T = 1 is the model as trained.
  • Low T makes the top word win more and lowers diversity. High T flattens the chances and leads to incoherent text. Holtzman et al. 2020, "The Curious Case of Neural Text Degeneration", Section 3 and Figure 3 (arXiv 1904.09751).
  • The wheel shows the top 3 words, then three groups that hold the exact chance of every other token: ranks 4-30, 31-300, and 301-50,257. When a group wins, you see one real word from that group (picked ahead of time, with a word filter). The real model could have written any word in the group.
  • "1 in N" = 1 divided by the chance GPT-2 gives your exact words at T = 1. The knob has 7 real settings; between them the bars slide smoothly (a blend, for looks). Chances are rounded to 0.1%.
  • The 3 level targets (1 in 10,000, 1 in a million, 1 in a billion) are game goals we picked, not statistics. The machine, mascot and sounds are drawings and our own sound design (sources in assets/SOUNDS.md).
