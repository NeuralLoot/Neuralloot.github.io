Word map: Look = visual feedback · Open-loop = fixed plan before seeing results · Closed-loop = look, then choose next atomic step · DONE = completion token · Frozen executor = planner correct, executor does not adapt.

The paper. WorldGuide (arXiv 2610.12459) is a goal-directed video world model for procedural tasks. It plans and executes atomic steps with visual feedback and memory, ending with a DONE token. On WorldGuide Bench (~59K / 58,679 step-annotated videos, 245 tasks, 27 categories) Task Success is 33.33% vs MiniMax-H3 29.90% even though MiniMax gets reference plans. On Video-CraftBench, 47.69% vs 32.73%. Closed-loop beats open-loop 33.33% vs 11.71%. Visual feedback beats action-history-only 33.33% vs 14.72%. Memory adds +5.77 pp / +17.91 pp.

How the game maps to it. Level 1 shows open-loop drift, then closed-loop LOOK → PICK. Level 2 adds distractor steps (skip/repeat fails). Level 3 gives a correct plan but a frozen executor that ignores vision unless you LOOK before each pick — the planner+executor gap.

Toy parts. Pan art, clip timing, miss budgets, and recipes are hand-written. Numbers on the research card are from the paper only.
