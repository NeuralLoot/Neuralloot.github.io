🤓 Nerd notes · what the game teaches (for researchers)

What the game teaches

Meta plots each model's score on tasks it targeted in post-training (x) against tasks it didn't (y). Within one model the dots follow a straight trend, so you can predict the held-out score. Wonder Full breaks Muse Spark 1.3's trend: at about 62% on trained-on tasks it scores about 42% held-out, where Spark's trend says about 34% (traced, approximate). Meta credits the larger base model, and that is the experiment in level 2: thinking longer slides a model along its own line, while a stronger base lifts the whole line. Level 3 hands you the yardstick: on Meta's own Personal Superintelligence Index Wonder Full leads Claude Opus 5.5, 59.1 to 55.2, and on SWE-Marathon 57.5 to 46.3; on the Artificial Analysis Index Opus leads 58 to 56, and on ProgramBench 70.5 to 59.0.
