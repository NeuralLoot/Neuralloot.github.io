Last round, in real terms: Play a level to see the expert note here.

Word map: Long-horizon = many turns before you stop · Proxy = a score that can trick you · Leave-one-out = hide one view and recheck · RSI = keep revising under a budget · Submit = claim the job is done.

The paper. TerminalHorizon (arXiv 2610.13166) scales agentic data for long-horizon terminal intelligence: 1,535 task environments, 2,852 training trajectories (avg 52.8 turns). TerminalHorizon-27B lifts TB 2.0 to 55.8 (+14.2) and TB 2.1 to 60.67 (+13.85). AutomationBench Pass +11.7 / Partial +23.3. An RSI case under a 12h budget gained +27.03 on a hidden multi-view score vs a base run that quit at 12.1 min on a bad proxy.

How the game maps to it. Level 1 punishes one-command Submit (short traj). Level 2 requires Explore→Implement→Verify→Leave-one-out under a budget clock. Level 3 shows a glowing proxy that looks great while leave-one-view-out fails until you Revise (RSI).

Toy parts. Station layout, turn costs, and clock lengths are hand-written. Research-card numbers are from the paper only.
