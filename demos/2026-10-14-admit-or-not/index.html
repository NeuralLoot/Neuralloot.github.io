Word map: Harness = scaffolding around the model (prompts, memory, workflows, tools) with weights FIXED · Open / Admit = keep a revision after a fresh validation check · Fresh check = a new test not seen in training · HarnessOpt (HO) = optimize the harness · SkillOpt = optimize skills only · EIP = evolving-in-production spreadsheet case.

The paper. Self-evolving Agent Harness (arXiv 2610.18813; Harness-RSI). Model weights stay fixed while the harness revises from feedback. On SpreadsheetBench (Table 2 / SkillOpt-Lite study): GPT-5.4-nano HarnessOpt+skill reaches 77.58%, exceeding GPT-5.5 + SkillOpt at 76.20%. HO+skill lifts all four models +5.69–11.39 pp over SkillOpt-Lite. EIP spreadsheet case: cumulative savings by task 5 of −110,351 tokens / −22.97 s. Admission needs fresh independent validation; the paper distinguishes interpreted-state vs executable revisions.

How the game maps to it. You are the vault gatekeeper. Each evidence card shows train vs fresh clues. Open the door only when fresh check passes and old tasks do not regress. Door 2 forces the SpreadsheetBench lesson: open for the nano harness upgrade (77.58), seal a weight fine-tune (weights are FIXED). Door 3 chains admissions so spam-OPEN without fresh checks cracks the vault.

Skeptic. A smaller model + better harness can beat a bigger model on one bench — that is not automatic RSI. Bad admissions without fresh checks regress.
