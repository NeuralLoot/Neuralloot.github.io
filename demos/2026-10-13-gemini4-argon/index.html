Level 2 follows the Argon for Robotics post. Real2Sim prompt rule "No sim cheating": objects move only through real MuJoCo contact (mj_step). Banned after t = 0: external forces/torques, welds or equality constraints, setting qpos/qvel, disabling collisions, contact forces not from the solver (e.g. copying logged tactile readings). From one RGB photo Argon built a MuJoCo twin, a 77-task curriculum and 4,500+ episodes; VLA fine-tuning raised real success 56% → 75% (35 s → 24 s). ERQA embodied reasoning: 87%.

Toy parts: the cheat events, timing, and the "clean practice → real robot %" meter (your catch rate scaled onto Google's 56→75 range) are game logic, not Google's scoring.

What's real

Gemini 4 Argon came out Oct 13, 2026. Google's own table shows it is not best at everything. It wins long code fixes (DeepSWE), giant maps of text (GraphWalks) and long videos (LVBench). It comes last of four on command-line jobs (Terminal-bench) and the hardest coding puzzles (FrontierSWE). For robots, Argon builds a sim (a pretend physics world) from one photo. The rule there: no sim cheating.
