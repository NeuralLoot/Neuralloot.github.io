🤓 Nerd notes real terms, numbers, method

What you just played. In RealtimeBench, an AI reads a text description of the game, picks a legal move, and the game keeps running with the previous control still active while it thinks. Your arrow press is the AI's answer: on the live clock it lands a few moves after you saw the board, at the glowing ring.

Word map. Response latency L = tresponse − tobserve · Action age A = tapply − tobserve (latency plus waiting for the next game tick; outcomes follow the state at tapply) · Clocks = Paused (game frozen during inference), Current (the game's own live pace), 1.5× and 2× faster · Decoy food = food placed where chasing it is fatal · TORSI (Two-Timescale Offline Recursive Self-Improvement) = a fast student (Jev) acts alone during play; a slow teacher (GPT-6.1-sol, medium effort) studies the student's failures between attempts, tests fixes in paused replays, and writes reusable guidance; a new version is adopted only if its validation score is strictly higher.
